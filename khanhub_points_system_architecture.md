# KhanHub Staff Performance & Points Distribution System

> **Version:** 2.0 — Dual-Tier Gamified Architecture  
> **Target Platform:** KhanHub Ecosystem (`apps/web` Monorepo)  
> **Last Updated:** August 2026  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

The **KhanHub Points Distribution System** is designed to drive operational excellence, boost staff engagement, eliminate subjectivity in evaluations, and foster healthy internal competition across all KhanHub departments (Hospital, Rehab, SPIMS, IT, Social Media, Job Center, Welfare, Sukoon Center, and Official HQ).

By combining **daily operational discipline** with **growth contributions**, the system rewards high performers with financial bonuses, badges, and career progression while providing early warnings and corrective paths for lagging personnel.

---

## 2. Current Points Breakdown & Scoring Mechanics

Every staff member is evaluated daily across **four core pillars**. Each pillar yields up to **1 mark per day** (maximum 30 marks per month per category).

| Pillar | Daily Max | Monthly Max | Scoring Rule & Evaluation Criteria |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 📅 **Attendance** | 1 Mark | 30 Marks | **1 Mark:** Arrived on or before shift start time.<br>**0 Marks:** Late arrival, unexcused absence, or missing punch. |
| 👔 **Uniform & Appearance** | 1 Mark | 30 Marks | **1 Mark:** 100% compliance with assigned dress code (Shoes, Overall/Lab Coat, ID Card, Hijab/Cap, etc.).<br>**0 Marks:** Any single required uniform item missing. |
| 📋 **Working / Task Completion** | 1 Mark | 30 Marks | **1 Mark:** All assigned daily duties and checklists completed.<br>**0 Marks:** Any assigned daily duty left incomplete or unverified. |
| 🚀 **Growth Points** | 1 Mark | 30 Marks | **1 Mark:** Submitted at least 1 verified organizational improvement, marketing idea, process optimization, or client referral.<br>**0 Marks:** No growth contribution submitted. |
| **TOTAL** | **4 Marks / Day** | **120 Marks / Month** | **Maximum Achievable Monthly Score: 120 Points** |

## 3. Dual-Tier Competition Architecture (Staff vs. Admin)

### 💡 Why Separate Competitions?

Evaluating junior staff (e.g., sweepers, receptionists, junior nurses) on the exact same scale as senior administrators creates structural unfairness. Admins have higher decision-making scope, while ground staff focus on physical task execution. 

To create a **fair, motivating, and transparent environment**, KhanHub introduces **Two Separate Monthly Leagues**:

---

```
                       ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                       │    KHANHUB COMPETITION ARCHITECTURE     │
                       └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                            │
                   ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
                   ▼                                                 ▼
     ┌───────────────────────────┐                     ┌───────────────────────────┐
     │   LEAGUE A: STAFF LEVEL   │                     │   LEAGUE B: ADMIN LEVEL   │
     │  (Operations & Execution) │                     │  (Management & Strategy)  │
     └─────────────┬─────────────┘                     └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                   │                                                 │
     • Junior, Staff, Worker, Nurse                    • Senior, Lead, Manager, Admin
     • Focus: Daily Discipline                         • Focus: Team SLA, Audit, Growth
     • Prize: Cash Bonus + Perks                       • Prize: Leadership Bonus + Perks
```

---

### 🟢 League A: Staff Level Competition (Operations & Ground Execution)

#### **Eligible Seniority Levels & Roles:**
- **Junior**, **Staff**, **Worker**, **Internee**, **Trial**, **Contract**
- Roles: Assistant Nurses, Ward Staff, Receptionists, Office Boys, Security Guards, Sweepers, Lab Assistants, Instructors.

#### **Evaluation Metrics & Weights:**
1. **Punctuality & Shift Compliance (30%)**: Zero late arrivals.
2. **Uniform Perfection (30%)**: Pristine dress code execution daily.
3. **Daily Task Completion (30%)**: 100% duty checklist done.
4. **Growth Suggestions & Helpfulness (10%)**: Submitting simple process improvement ideas or peer support.

#### **League A Monthly Leaderboard & Rewards:**
- 🥇 **1st Place:** Rs. 6,000 + "Staff of the Month" Plaque + 1 Paid Day Off
- 🥈 **2nd Place:** Rs. 3,500 + Shield of Honor
- 🥉 **3rd Place:** Rs. 2,000 + Commendation Letter
- 🎖️ **Top 10%:** "Perfectionist" Digital Badge on Profile & Dashboard

---

### 🔵 League B: Admin & Management Competition (Leadership & Strategy)

#### **Eligible Seniority Levels & Roles:**
- **Senior**, **Lead**, **Managerial**, **Executive**, **Admin**, **Superadmin**
- Roles: Department Administrators, IT Lead, Finance/Cashier Managers, Head Psychologists, Senior OT Surgeons, HR Managers.

#### **Evaluation Metrics & Weights:**
1. **Team SLA & Task Clearance Rate (35%)**: Overall completion rate of their department staff's daily duties and resolution speed of open issues.
2. **Operational Accuracy & Zero Fines (25%)**: Audit score of financial records, patient files, or inventory registers under their management.
3. **Strategic Growth Contributions (25%)**: Submitting high-impact growth proposals (revenue generation, cost reduction, new department features).
4. **Personal Discipline & Leadership (15%)**: Punctuality, uniform compliance, and team mentorship.

#### **League B Monthly Leaderboard & Rewards:**
- 🏆 **1st Place Manager:** Rs. 10,000 Leadership Bonus + Executive Trophy + Quarterly Strategy Dinner with CEO
- 🥈 **2nd Place Manager:** Rs. 6,000 Bonus + Leadership Certificate
- 🥉 **3rd Place Manager:** Rs. 3,500 Bonus
- 👑 **Department Shield:** The top-performing department receives the **"KhanHub Department of Excellence Banner"** to display at their main office entrance for the month.

## 4. Advanced Recommendations to Improve the Points System

To make the scoring system even more engaging, dynamic, and effective, we recommend implementing the following 5 strategic enhancements:

### 1. ⚡ Streak Multipliers & Consistency Bonuses
- **Concept:** Reward unbroken streaks of daily perfection to encourage long-term habits.
- **Rules:**
  - **7-Day Perfect Streak:** +3 Bonus Growth Points.
  - **30-Day Unbroken Streak:** +15 Bonus Growth Points + "Unstoppable" Badge.

### 2. 🤝 Peer-to-Peer "Kudos" System
- **Concept:** Allow staff members to send 2 "Kudos" (appreciation points) per month to colleagues who helped them during emergency shifts or heavy workloads.
- **Impact:** Fosters teamwork and builds strong inter-departmental relationships.

### 3. 🛡️ Fine Mitigation & Repentance Tasks
- **Concept:** Instead of purely penalizing staff for accidental non-compliance, allow staff to erase small fines or missing point penalties by completing designated "Repentance Tasks" (e.g., extra inventory audit, mentoring a junior, organizing files).

### 4. 🎁 Points Store & Redemptions
- **Concept:** Accumulate long-term lifetime points that can be redeemed for tangible perks:
  - 100 Lifetime Points = Priority Shift Preference
  - 250 Lifetime Points = Half-day Pass
  - 500 Lifetime Points = Annual Fuel/Transit Voucher (Rs. 3,000)

### 5. 📊 Live Real-Time Leaderboard Widgets
- **Concept:** Display real-time, animated leaderboards on department TV screens and dashboard headers showing live ranks for both Staff and Admin leagues.
